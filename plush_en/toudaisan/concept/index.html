Toudai-san as a Concept

Toudai-san (Concept) is not modeled after any particular real-world lighthouse. This is Toudai-san born from the very idea of a lighthouse.

Unlike the Toudai-san characters inspired by lighthouses around the world, this one is something like the original form from which all Toudai-san characters emerge.

The creator first began drawing Toudai-san in high school, and they have been together ever since.

Not one particular lighthouse, but a Toudai-san born from “the lighthouse itself.”
