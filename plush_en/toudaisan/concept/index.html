Mr. Lighthouse as a Concept

Mr. Lighthouse (Concept) is not modeled after any particular real-world lighthouse. He was born from the very idea of a lighthouse.

Unlike the characters inspired by individual lighthouses around the world, this Mr. Lighthouse is something like the original form from which they all emerge.

The creator first began drawing Mr. Lighthouse in high school, and they have been together ever since.

Not one particular lighthouse, but Mr. Lighthouse born from the idea of “the lighthouse itself.”
