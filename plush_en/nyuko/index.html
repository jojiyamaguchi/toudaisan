A Catlike Creature That Is Not a Cat

Nyuko looks very much like a cat, but is not a cat. Nyuko is a divine messenger created by Nyx, goddess of the night.

Nyuko can change color freely, appearing black, white, brown, pale blue, green, and more. In ordinary feline colors, Nyuko is indistinguishable from a regular cat. You may only recognize Nyuko when the color turns blue or green.

Using “thought magic,” Nyuko sends images such as “I want fish” or “I want milk” to nearby people. Nyuko can also use “night magic” to make the surroundings a little darker.

Despite being a goddess’s messenger, Nyuko behaves and thinks much like a cat and is just as capricious. Nyuko hardly works at all.

A renowned mathematician once tried to calculate how many Nyuko were hiding among ordinary cats. But after one moved into his house, he was so content that he abandoned the research.

It might be a cat. It might be Nyuko.
